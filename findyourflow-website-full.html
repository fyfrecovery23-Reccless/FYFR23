Outdoor Recovery Therapy

Three hours outside
changes everything.

Outdoor recreation rebuilds the dopamine pathways that addiction damaged. Fishing, kayaking, and e-biking aren't just fun — they are evidence-based recovery tools that create community, identity, and a reason to stay sober every single day.

🎣
Fishing

Patience, presence, and peace. Fishing teaches sober dads to slow down and find flow in the quiet — skills that transfer directly to recovery.

🛶
Kayaking

Water therapy is real. Paddling together as a group builds brotherhood, reduces cortisol, and creates shared memories that don't require substances.

⚡
E-Bikes

Freedom, movement, and fun. E-bikes open Colorado's trails to men at every fitness level — making outdoor recovery accessible for everyone.

🏔️
Hiking

Colorado's mountains are a natural therapy room. Group hikes build community, physical strength, and the kind of perspective that only elevation can provide.

🛴
Scooters

Urban mobility and freedom. Scooters help clients get to work, meetings, and appointments while building independence in early recovery.

🌲
Nature Therapy

Structured time in nature — sitting by water, walking trails, grounding exercises outside. Simple, powerful, and available every single day.

"A man who finds joy in the outdoors has found one more reason not to use today. That's how recovery is built — one good morning at a time."

About Us

Find Your Flow
Recovery LLC

Find Your Flow Recovery LLC is a Colorado-based peer recovery coaching organization established December 6, 2025. We are founded and operated by a certified peer recovery coach who is in long-term sobriety and a father himself.

We exist because fathers in recovery are underserved. Most recovery programming is designed around women and mothers. The courts, the treatment centers, the halfway houses — they were not built with fathers in mind. Find Your Flow Recovery LLC was built for them. By one of them.

Our ecosystem includes peer-led one-on-one coaching, the Overcomer recovery app, structured outdoor recreation therapy using fishing, kayaking, and e-biking, father-centered support groups, peer coach training for sober dads who want to guide others, and a planned certified sober living home for fathers in Colorado.

Every service we offer is built around one belief: the most powerful motivator for a man in recovery is his children. We build everything around that truth.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Find Your Flow Recovery LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Overcomer mobile application and this website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account
  • Recovery Data: Sobriety dates, trigger logs, mood check-ins, and activity data you voluntarily enter
  • Usage Data: App features used and session patterns (used only to improve the app)
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and unique identifiers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Overcomer App and its features
  • To track your sobriety progress and trigger patterns as requested by you
  • To connect you with accountability partners (premium members only, with your consent)
  • To improve your experience and send important app updates

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

Your recovery data is sensitive. We use industry-standard encryption and we never sell your personal data. Your sobriety and recovery information is never shared with employers, insurance companies, courts, or any other party without your explicit written consent, except as required by law.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We share information only: with your explicit consent, to comply with law, or to protect safety. We will notify you before any business transfer.

5. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time
  • Export your recovery data
  • Remove accountability partners at any time
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

6. Children's Privacy

The Overcomer App is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy with a new date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
